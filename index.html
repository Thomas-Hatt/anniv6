Our Unfolding Story

The Spark

We met at a time when I needed someone genuine the most, and you've always been that, even on day one. You've showed me love and affection, even on days when I didn't deserve it. I genuinely couldn't ask for a better boyfriend who cares about me, my progression, and our future. Even on bad days, I love you more than anything. You've always completed me and improved my outlook on life. I'm looking forward to what this new year will bring us, and I hope to continue to spend many more years together.

First Date

I still vividly remember our first date. I was so nervous that I barely knew what to say, but I enjoyed just listening to you talk. Even then, you were weird and different from anyone else I had ever been with, and that's what I loved about you. You made me feel calm and captivated at the same time. As I look back now, that evening wasn't just a date; it was the exact moment I realized I had found someone truly special.


Following Dates

Every following date was uniquely special, and I remember so many small details from each one. It was so unique seeing you every time, especially since you weren't able to drive your car over to my house yet. Those early days of asking for rides created a distinct intimacy. We had to be more creative and deliberate with our time, which meant we focused less on the destination and more on just being together. That effort that we put in truly set us apart and showed me early on how much you valued making time for me.


Looking Ahead

I cannot wait to see what our future holds for us. I love you, and I'll always cherish what you have done for me in this life no matter what. I'll never regret being with you, even through dark times.
